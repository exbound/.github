# Governance

Exbound is founder-led and deliberately lightweight. This document says how
decisions get made and how responsibility is earned, so the project can grow
without bureaucracy.

## Roles

- **Maintainers** hold write access, review and merge pull requests, and are
  responsible for a repository's direction and quality. The organization is
  founder-led; the founder is the final maintainer for cross-cutting decisions.
- **Contributors** are anyone who opens an issue or pull request. No special
  access is required to contribute.

## Earning write access

Write access follows a track record, not a request: several merged,
evidence-backed contributions to a repository; adherence to the contribution and
evidence standards; and sound review judgment on others' work. A maintainer
proposes the change and the founder confirms it. Access is scoped to the
repositories where you've done the work.

## Decisions

- Routine technical choices are made in the pull request or issue that raises
  them.
- Decisions that set a convention across repositories, change the scope
  boundary, or affect a public contract are recorded as a short decision note in
  the relevant repository, so the reasoning survives.
- When maintainers disagree, the founder decides, and the reasoning is written
  down.

## What we are not

No foundation, elected board, committees, or formal voting. If the project ever
outgrows founder-led governance, that change will itself be a recorded decision,
made in the open.
