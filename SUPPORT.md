# Support

Exbound is a volunteer open-source project. Here's where different kinds of
questions go.

- **A bug in Exbound code** — open an issue in the repository it affects, using
  the bug template.
- **A research question, a target nomination, or "how do I start?"** —
  [research](https://github.com/exbound/research): its Discussions, or an issue
  there.
- **A security or scope concern** — follow [SECURITY](SECURITY.md); don't open a
  public issue.
- **How to contribute** — [CONTRIBUTING](CONTRIBUTING.md), then a repository's
  own `CONTRIBUTING.md` and `AGENTS.md`.

## Private contact

For security issues, conduct reports, or anything that shouldn't be public, use
the affected repository's private reporting — the **"Report a vulnerability"**
button on its **Security** tab — or contact a maintainer directly. Please don't
open a public issue for these.

## What we can't help with

Using, licensing, or troubleshooting the proprietary products themselves.
Exbound builds independent, compatible software; it is not vendor support and is
not affiliated with any vendor.
